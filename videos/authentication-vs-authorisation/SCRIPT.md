## Line 1 — The door (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 8.0s
**Delivery:** Flat and level. The third sentence is the payload — land "which doors
open" and stop.

    Walk up to a hotel room door. Your keycard proves who you are. It does not decide which doors open.

## Line 2 — The front desk (Frame 3)

**Time:** 13.0 – 24.5s
**Delivery:** Brisk through the exchange, then slow on the definition. The three

    Start at the front desk. Hand over a passport. They check it, then they issue a card. Authentication is that whole exchange — a claim, a check, a credential.

## Line 3 — The lock (Frame 4)

**Time:** 24.5 – 36.0s
**Delivery:** This is the film's thesis. Slow the middle. A beat of silence before

## Line 4 — The naming trap (Frame 5)

**Time:** 36.0 – 47.5s
**Delivery:** Sharper. This is a correction, and the narrator knows it will be

    Read the two status codes carefully. Four oh one says Unauthorized, but it means we do not know you. Four oh three means we know you, and the answer is no.
